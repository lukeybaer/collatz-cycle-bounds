# Priority review: 2–3 October 2026

## Conclusion and limits

I found no matching statement of the specific all-orbit bound with `lambda = log2(3) - 1/80`, its displayed prefactor, or an exclusion of the same local-minimum cases 96–100, in the sources searched below. That supports a candidate contribution; it does **not** establish priority. This review was performed by the same AI system that developed the manuscript, not an independent specialist.

The defensible novelty claim is narrow: the particular explicit specialization and its consequences. P-adic logarithm bounds, normalized exponents, cycle inequalities, parity coding, convex extremal arguments and pruning are established methods. No separate novelty claim is made for the general nonlinear majorization lemma. Correctness and novelty are different questions, and neither follows from a passing program.

Our first public edition dates to 29 September 2026. This revision is not journal acceptance. The located Wang record is a preprint dated 29 July 2026; an author-uploaded document with an INTEGERS-style heading and placeholder publication fields is not evidence of acceptance.

## Remaining ways the priority claim could fail

1. An older result in different parameters may imply the same or a stronger bound after a non-obvious translation.
2. A specialist may recognize the explicit improvement as a routine corollary, reducing its significance even if the constants are absent from prior papers.
3. A recent, unindexed or inaccessible preprint may contain the same finite cases or a stronger envelope.
4. An error in the specialization or computational reduction could invalidate the result before priority matters.

The cheapest useful external review is a narrow request: compare the exact statement and parameter dictionary with the closest literature, then inspect the Bugeaud specialization and restart argument. No literature search can guarantee the absence of prior work. The present revision improves traceability and narrows the claim; it does not declare the priority question settled.
